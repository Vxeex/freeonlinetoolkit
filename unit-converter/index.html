How to Use the Unit Converter

Select a category (length, weight, volume, temperature, area, or speed), then choose the units you want to convert from and to. Enter the value and click Convert to see the result instantly.

The converter supports both metric and imperial units across all categories. Common conversions include: meters to feet, kilograms to pounds, liters to gallons, Celsius to Fahrenheit, and kilometers per hour to miles per hour.

Results are displayed with up to 4 decimal places for precision. The conversion formulas used are based on standard international definitions.

Conversion Reference

Length: 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 mile = 1.609 km

Weight: 1 kg = 2.20462 lbs, 1 ounce = 28.3495 g, 1 stone = 6.35029 kg

Volume: 1 gallon (US) = 3.78541 L, 1 quart = 0.946353 L, 1 fl oz = 29.5735 mL

Temperature: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9, °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Area: 1 sq ft = 0.092903 m², 1 acre = 4046.86 m², 1 hectare = 10,000 m²

Speed: 1 mph = 1.60934 km/h, 1 knot = 1.852 km/h
