How to Use

Select your gender, then enter your height and circumference measurements. Measure your neck just below the larynx, your waist at navel level, and your hips at the widest point (women only). Use a flexible tape measure and keep it snug but not tight.

This calculator uses the U.S. Navy circumference method, one of the most widely used body fat estimation formulas. It has an accuracy of about ±2–3% compared to hydrostatic weighing.

Frequently Asked Questions

The U.S. Navy method has a margin of error of about ±2–3% compared to hydrostatic weighing. It's more accurate than BMI for assessing body composition and is widely used by military and fitness professionals.
BMI uses only height and weight, so it can misclassify muscular people as overweight. Body fat percentage directly measures how much of your weight is fat vs. lean mass, giving a more accurate picture of your fitness level.
