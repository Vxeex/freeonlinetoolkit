How to Use the Time Zone Converter

Select your source time zone (the time zone you're converting from) and your target time zone (the time zone you want to convert to). Choose a date and time, then click Convert to see the equivalent time in your target zone.

Click the Now button to automatically set the current date and time in your local time zone. The converter handles Daylight Saving Time (DST) transitions automatically based on standard UTC offsets for each time zone.

The results show the converted time, the source and target times side by side, and the time difference between the two zones (e.g., "+3 hours" or "-8 hours").

Time Zone Reference

Time zones are typically expressed as UTC offsets (e.g., UTC+5:30 for India Standard Time). Major time zones include:

  • UTC / GMT ??Coordinated Universal Time (Western Europe, UK in winter)
  • EST / EDT ??Eastern Time (New York, Toronto) ??UTC-5/UTC-4
  • CST / CDT ??Central Time (Chicago, Mexico City) ??UTC-6/UTC-5
  • PST / PDT ??Pacific Time (Los Angeles, Vancouver) ??UTC-8/UTC-7
  • CET / CEST ??Central European Time (Paris, Berlin) ??UTC+1/UTC+2
  • IST ??India Standard Time (Mumbai, New Delhi) ??UTC+5:30
  • JST ??Japan Standard Time (Tokyo, Seoul) ??UTC+9
  • AEST / AEDT ??Australian Eastern Time (Sydney, Melbourne) ??UTC+10/UTC+11
