How to Use the Currency Converter

Using this currency converter is simple. Select the source currency (the currency you're converting from) and the target currency (the currency you're converting to) from the dropdown menus. Enter the amount you want to convert, and the calculator instantly shows the converted value using live exchange rates.

Need to reverse the conversion? Click the Swap button to switch the source and target currencies. This is useful when checking both directions of a currency pair.

Exchange rates are fetched from a free API and may be slightly delayed. For large transactions, check with your bank or currency exchange service for the most current rates.

Common Use Cases

  • Travel budgeting: Convert your home currency to your destination's currency to plan expenses
  • Online shopping: Check the real cost of international purchases in your own currency
  • International transfers: Estimate how much the recipient will get after conversion
  • Business: Calculate costs and pricing for international transactions
