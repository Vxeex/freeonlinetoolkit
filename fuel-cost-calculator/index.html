How to Use the Fuel Cost Calculator

Planning a road trip or just wondering about your commute costs? This fuel cost calculator gives you an accurate estimate. Choose between US units (miles, MPG, dollars per gallon) or metric units (kilometers, liters per 100km, dollars per liter).

Enter the total distance of your trip, your vehicle's fuel efficiency (check your owner's manual or the EPA rating), and the current fuel price at your local station. The calculator shows the total fuel cost, the amount of fuel you'll need, and the cost per mile or kilometer.

For example, a 300-mile trip in a car that gets 25 MPG with gas at $3.50/gallon will cost about $42 in fuel. Use this to budget your trip or compare the fuel costs of different vehicles.

Fuel Cost Formula

US: Cost = (Distance / MPG) × Price per Gallon
Metric: Cost = (Distance × L/100km / 100) × Price per Liter
