How to Use

Select whether you want to calculate based on your last menstrual period (LMP) or conception date. If using LMP, enter the first day of your last period and your average cycle length. If using conception, enter the date of conception. Click Calculate to see your due date, current pregnancy week, trimester, and more.

The calculator uses Naegele's rule: 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your LMP, adjusted for cycle length. For conception dates, it adds 266 days (38 weeks).

Frequently Asked Questions
