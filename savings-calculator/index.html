How to Use the Savings Calculator

This calculator helps you project how your savings will grow over time. Start by entering your initial deposit ??the amount you already have saved. Then add your monthly contribution to see how regular saving adds up.

Enter the annual interest rate your savings account or investment offers, and the time period in years. Choose how often interest compounds ??monthly, quarterly, or annually. The more frequently interest compounds, the faster your money grows.

Click Calculate to see your total balance, total contributions, and total interest earned. Adjust any value to see how changes affect your savings ??try increasing your monthly contribution or extending the time period.

Savings Formula

The future value of a series of savings with compound interest is calculated as:

FV = P(1+r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1+r/n)^(nt) ??1) / (r/n)]

Where: FV = future value, P = initial deposit, PMT = monthly contribution, r = annual interest rate, n = compounds per year, and t = time in years.

Frequently Asked Questions

A common guideline is to save 20% of your income, but any amount is better than none. Start with what you can afford and increase over time. Use this calculator to experiment with different monthly amounts and see the long-term impact.
High-yield savings accounts typically offer 4-5% APY. Money market accounts and CDs may offer similar or slightly higher rates. For long-term goals, investment accounts may offer higher returns but come with more risk.
More frequent compounding means your money grows faster because interest is calculated on a larger balance more often. Monthly compounding earns more than quarterly, which earns more than annual compounding ??especially over long time periods.
This calculator focuses on regular monthly contributions to a savings goal. The Compound Interest Calculator focuses more on investment growth with variable compounding periods and no required monthly contributions.
