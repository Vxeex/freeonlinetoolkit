How to Use the Tip Calculator

Using this tip calculator is quick and straightforward. Enter your bill amount (pre-tax or post-tax — whichever you prefer to tip on), the tip percentage you'd like to leave, and how many people are splitting the bill.

Use the quick-tip buttons for standard tipping percentages (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%), or enter a custom percentage in the input field. The calculator instantly shows the tip amount, the total bill with tip, and the per-person breakdown.

This is especially useful for group dinners where you want to split everything evenly. No more awkward math at the table — just enter the numbers and see exactly what each person owes.

Standard Tipping Guidelines

  • Restaurants / Dining: 15-20% of the pre-tax bill
  • Delivery Drivers: 10-15% or $3-5 minimum
  • Bar / Bartenders: $1-2 per drink or 15-20% of the tab
  • Hair Salon / Spa: 15-25% of the service cost
  • Hotel Staff: $2-5 per night for housekeeping
  • Taxi / Rideshare: 10-15% of the fare

Frequently Asked Questions

Standard etiquette suggests tipping on the pre-tax amount. However, many people tip on the total bill (post-tax) for simplicity. Either is generally acceptable — the difference is usually small.
While tipping is customary for good service, you can adjust the percentage down for poor service. Many people leave 10% for below-average service. If service was truly unacceptable, speak to the manager rather than just leaving no tip.
