How to Use

Select your gender and enter your height in either centimeters or feet/inches. The calculator will compute your ideal weight using four standard medical formulas. The consensus range shows the minimum and maximum across all four results.

Important: Ideal weight formulas are estimates. They don't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. Use them as a general reference, not a strict target.
