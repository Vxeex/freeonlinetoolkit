How to Use

Enter the first day of your last period, your average cycle length, and your typical luteal phase length (default 14 days). The calculator will determine your ovulation day and fertile window — the 6-day period when conception is most likely.

Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period. The fertile window includes the 5 days before ovulation plus ovulation day itself.

Frequently Asked Questions
