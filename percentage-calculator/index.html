How to Use the Percentage Calculator

Choose the type of calculation you need from the dropdown menu:

  • What is X% of Y? — Find a percentage of a number (e.g., 20% of 200 = 40)
  • X is what % of Y? — Find what percent one number is of another (e.g., 30 is 20% of 150)
  • Percentage increase/decrease — Calculate the percentage change between two values

Enter your numbers and click Calculate to see the result instantly. The calculator also shows the formula and intermediate steps for each calculation type.

Percentage Formulas

Find X% of Y:

Result = (X ÷ 100) × Y

X is what % of Y:

Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Percentage change:

Change = ((New - Original) ÷ Original) × 100
