How to Use the VAT Calculator

This VAT calculator handles both adding and removing VAT from any amount. Enter the amount you're working with, select the applicable VAT rate for your country (or enter a custom one), and choose whether you want to add VAT (calculate the gross amount including tax) or remove VAT (extract the net amount before tax from a gross figure).

VAT Calculation Formula

Add VAT: Total = Net × (1 + Rate/100)
Remove VAT: Net = Gross / (1 + Rate/100)

Frequently Asked Questions

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax placed on products and services at each stage of the supply chain. It's used in over 160 countries, including all EU member states, the UK, Canada (GST/HST), Australia (GST), and many others.
Standard VAT rates vary by country and product type. Most goods and services use the standard rate (19-27% depending on country). Reduced rates apply to essentials like food, children's clothing, and books. Check your country's tax authority website for specific rates.
VAT and sales tax are different. Sales tax is collected only at the final point of sale to the consumer. VAT is collected at each stage of production and distribution. Both achieve similar economic results but through different mechanisms.
